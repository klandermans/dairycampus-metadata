Dairy Campus Research Data Platform: Standardized Metadata Dictionaries, Extraction Queries, and Calculation Models

Authors: Bert Klandermans & Fedde de Jong
Institution: Dairy Campus, Wageningen University & Research (Leeuwarden, the Netherlands)

1. Overview

This deposit provides the authoritative FAIR metadata dictionaries, data definitions, and calculation algorithms for high-throughput dairy cattle research data collected at the Dairy Campus research facility. It documents 19 core research tables comprising 507 scientific variables across cattle nutrition, emissions, milk production, and animal behavior.

2. Interactive Metadata Tables (Included Files)

To inspect and search the data dictionaries directly in your browser, click Preview on the uploaded files:

  • 01_Dairy_Campus_Master_Data_Dictionary.csv: Complete searchable catalog of all 507 parameters with English labels, data types, physical measurement units, and calculation logic. Zenodo displays this as an interactive web table.
  • 02_Dairy_Campus_Metadata_Dictionary_EN.xlsx: Formatted multi-tab spreadsheet with an index sheet and dedicated sheets for each of the 19 research datasets.
  • 03_Dairy_Campus_Complete_Data_Catalog.html & .md: Complete standalone human-readable reference catalogs.
  • 04_Dairy_Campus_Calculations_and_Queries.zip: SQL calculation procedures, view definitions, and trial extraction queries.

4. Key Calculation Models & MPR Formulas

  • Dry Matter Intake (DMI): Calculated by subtracting mixer wagon added water from roughage intake and multiplying by weekly laboratory oven dry matter percentage:
    tot.opnDs = sum(Intake * ((TotalLoaded - AddedWaterKg) / TotalLoaded) * (DM_pct / 100))
  • Milk Recording (MPR) AO Yield-Weighted Formula:
    Component_daily = (Evening_Concentration * Evening_Yield + Morning_Concentration * Morning_Yield) / (Evening_Yield + Morning_Yield)
  • Fat- and Protein-Corrected Milk (FPCM / Standaardmelk):
    FPCM = MilkYield * (0.337 + 0.116 * Fat% + 0.060 * Protein%)
  • Feed Conversion Efficiency:
    FCE = FPCM (kg/day) / Dry Matter Intake (kg DM/day)
