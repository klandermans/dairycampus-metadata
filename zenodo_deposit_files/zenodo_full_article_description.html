Abstract

The Dairy Campus research facility (Wageningen University & Research, Leeuwarden, The Netherlands) operates an internationally leading experimental dairy infrastructure tracking an experimental herd of approximately 500 Holstein Friesian dairy cows continuously since 2016. This comprehensive data descriptor formalizes the multi-sensor, multi-lactation research database comprising 19 distinct research datasets and 507 scientific variables. Spanning more than a decade of automated observations, the platform contains millions of individual milking records (yields, electrical conductivity, milking duration, live cow weights), millions of electronic roughage intake visits recorded via load-cell troughs, continuous enteric greenhouse gas fluxes (CH4 and CO2), high-frequency behavioral accelerometry, and routine laboratory milk component analyses. All datasets are formally modeled as standardized distributions (dct:distribution) of a single central catalog under W3C DCAT and DataCite metadata standards.

1. Longitudinal Experimental Infrastructure (500 Dairy Cows, 2016–Present)

Dairy Campus in Leeuwarden is the national research and innovation center for dairy farming in the Netherlands, operated by Wageningen University & Research (WUR). Since 2016, Dairy Campus maintains an active experimental herd of approximately 500 high-producing Holstein Friesian dairy cows across dedicated research compartments and cubicle housing (stallen 50–73). The herd is continuously monitored throughout complete productive lifespans, encompassing multi-lactation trajectories, transition periods, and dry periods.

The longitudinal data architecture ingests and validates continuous telemetry streams from precision livestock farming (PLF) hardware:

  • Automated Milking Systems & Rotaries (GEA): Millions of individual milking transaction records. Each milking logs gross milk yield (kg), session duration, quarter-level milk electrical conductivity, blood presence detection, and cow live body weight measured on integrated walkthrough platform scales.
  • Individual Roughage Intake Control (Hokofarm RIC Troughs): Bins mounted on high-precision electronic load cells with integrated radio-frequency transponder recognition. Records individual visit timestamps (start/end), trough mass differences (intake per visit), and consumption rates across 4 distinct roughage fractions (rv1–rv4).
  • Automated Feeding Kitchen & Robot (Trioliet Triomatic): Tracks exact ingredient batch mixing, silage loading, added water mass, and discharge distributions along feed fences and individual RIC troughs.
  • Computerized Concentrate Feeding Stations (SAC & Hokofarm): Programmed individual concentrate allowances (types A, B, C, D) versus actual consumed quantities, dispenser speeds, and visit counts.
  • Enteric Greenhouse Gas Monitoring Hoods (C-Lock GreenFeed): Automated bait-fed breath analysis chambers measuring individual enteric methane (CH4) and carbon dioxide (CO2) emission fluxes during voluntary cow visits.
  • Continuous Behavioral Accelerometers (Nedap Velos Smarttags): High-resolution neck-collar and leg sensors classifying 24-hour time budgets into eating, ruminating, inactivity (lying), and active standing/walking.
  • On-Site Meteorological Weather Station: Continuous climate logging of ambient temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, solar radiation, and rainfall, linked to automated barn ventilation curtains.
  • Official Milk Laboratory Recording (CRV/MPR): Routine 4-weekly and trial-specific AO (evening and morning) sampling for fat %, protein %, lactose %, somatic cell count (SCC), urea content, and ketosis diagnostic indicators.

3. Mathematical Calculation Methodologies in SQL Layer

This section formalizes the core scientific calculations implemented directly in the database views and stored procedures defining the 19 research datasets.

3.1 Roughage Intake per Visit and per Day (Hokofarm RIC Troughs)

Hokofarm RIC troughs are mounted on load cells. The fresh roughage intake Iv and visit duration Δtv of individual visit v, and total daily fresh intake Ifresh, f, d for fraction f ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4} (columns rv1.Prod–rv4.Prod in view tvl_totaal) are computed as: 

  (1)   I_v = M_v,start - M_v,end
  (2)   Δt_v = t_v,end - t_v,start
  (3)   I_fresh,f,d = ∑_{v ∈ V_f,d} I_v

Definitions:
• Mv,start, Mv,end: Trough mass before cow entry and after exit (kg).
• tv,start, tv,end: Timestamps of visit entry and departure.
• Vf,d: Set of all completed feeding visits to troughs delivering roughage fraction f on day d.
• SQL Objects: t_raw_hokofarmvr, tvl_totaal.

3.2 Water Exclusion Deduction (Trioliet Triomatic Feeding Robot)

During total mixed ration (TMR) preparation in the Trioliet Triomatic automated feeding kitchen, water is added to suppress dust and prevent ration sorting. Fresh intake recorded by Hokofarm RIC bins is adjusted by deducting the proportion of added water logged in the robot load files (view VL_Totaal_sub_ric_opn_prod): 

                ∑ M_loaded - ∑ M_water
  (4)   R_water = -----------------------
                      ∑ M_loaded

  (5)   I_excl_water,f,d = I_fresh,f,d × R_water

Definitions:
• Rwater: Net dry-feed fraction ratio after deducting added water.
• Mloaded: Total batch mass loaded into Trioliet Triomatic mixer wagon (kg).
• Mwater: Mass of water added to batch during mixing (kg).
• Iexcl_water, f, d: Daily roughage intake excluding added water (columns rv1.ProdExclWater–rv4.ProdExclWater).
• SQL View: VL_Totaal_sub_ric_opn_prod.

3.3 Laboratory Dry Matter Content & Total Daily DMI

Weekly forage and silage samples are analyzed in the drying oven laboratory (droogstoof, VL_totaal_droogstoof) to determine gravimetric dry matter percentages (function WeekGemiddelde_DS). Total daily Dry Matter Intake (DMI) across all roughage fractions is: 

                  4
  (6)   DMI_d = ∑ [ I_excl_water,f,d × (DM_%,f / 100) ]
                 f=1

Definitions:
• DMId: Total daily dry matter intake (column tot.opnDs, kg DM/day).
• DM%, f: Laboratory gravimetric dry matter percentage of roughage fraction f.
• SQL Sources: VL_totaal_droogstoof, WeekGemiddelde_DS, tvl_totaal.

3.4 Milk Recording (MPR) Yield-Weighted Composite Concentrations

Milk samples are collected during evening (A) and morning (O) milking sessions (AO sampling). Weekly composite concentrations across test sessions in ISO-calendar week w are calculated as yield-weighted averages in view VL_Dier_Datum_Crv: 

               ∑_{j ∈ J_w} (c_j × Y_j)
  (7)   c̄_w = -------------------------
                  ∑_{j ∈ J_w} Y_j

Definitions:
• c̄w: Weekly yield-weighted mean concentration of component c ∈ {Fat%, Protein%, Lactose%, Urea, SCC}.
• cj: Concentration measured in milking session j.
• Yj: Milk yield recorded for session j (kg).
• Jw: Set of official test milkings recorded during calendar week w.
• SQL View: VL_Dier_Datum_Crv (columns mpr_week.* in tvl_totaal).

3.5 Animal Demographics & Lactation Timing

Standardized chronological and reproductive timing metrics are derived in view VL_totaal_bedrijf and table TVL_Dier_Datum_Stal: 

  (8)   DIM = t_record - t_calving                  (Days in Milk, LacDgn / IMDim)
  (9)   Age_days = t_record - t_birth               (Animal Age in Days, LftDgn / AgeDays)
  (10)  t_exp_calving = t_insemination + 280 days   (Expected Calving Date, VrwKlfDat)
  (11)  Days_to_calving = t_exp_calving - t_record  (Days Remaining to Calving, VrwDgn)

Definitions:
• DIM: Days in milk calculated from the most recent calving date.
• Agedays: Animal chronological age in days from official birth date.
• texp_calving: Projected calving date based on standard 280-day bovine gestation period.
• SQL Sources: tvl_totaal, T_RAW_Gea_milk, TVL_Dier_Datum_Stal.

6. Trial-Specific SQL Extraction Architecture

In the automated Apache Airflow data pipeline (report.py), research deliverables for a specific scientific study are extracted using parameterized SQL queries filtering by trial code (e.g., WHERE prfcod = '[prfcod]').

The core database architecture encompasses 30 production extraction queries (covering breeds, veterinary diagnoses, treatments, weighings, dry matter, barn climate, milking sessions, feeding visits, behavior summaries, and GreenFeed visits), along with 20 stored procedures, 190 views, and 26 user-defined functions that constitute the core calculation engine.
