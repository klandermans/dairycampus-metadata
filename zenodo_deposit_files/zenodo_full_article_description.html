Abstract

The Dairy Campus research facility (Wageningen University & Research, Leeuwarden, the Netherlands) operates a high-throughput, multi-sensor experimental dairy cattle infrastructure dedicated to precision livestock farming, sustainable dairy cattle nutrition, animal welfare, and enteric greenhouse gas mitigation. This comprehensive data descriptor provides the complete FAIR metadata documentation, operational parameters, algorithmic calculation models, and standardized data dictionaries for the Dairy Campus Research Data Platform. It elaborates 19 distinct research datasets comprising 507 scientific variables, detailing observation granularities, measurement units, sensor specifications, dry matter corrections, water exclusion deductions, milk component yield weighting, behavioral budgets, and automated quality control matrices.

1. Background & Experimental Facility

Dairy Campus is the preeminent national and international research, education, and innovation center for dairy farming in the Netherlands, operated by Wageningen University & Research (WUR). Dairy Campus manages an experimental herd of approximately 500 Holstein Friesian dairy cows housed in specialized compartments and cubicle pens (stallen 50–73). The facility is equipped with automated feeding kitchens, automated milking systems, computerized concentrate stations, high-frequency behavioral accelerometers, enteric methane and carbon dioxide measurement hoods, and continuous barn climate monitoring.

Scientific trials conducted at Dairy Campus investigate dairy cattle nutrition, feed efficiency, mitigation of enteric greenhouse gases (CH4 and CO2), precision health diagnostics, animal welfare, locomotion, and circular manure/nitrogen cycles. To support open science and the FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), this catalog formalizes the data models and calculation pipelines used to export experimental datasets.

2. Precision Livestock Farming (PLF) Sensor Infrastructure

The Dairy Campus data platform continuously ingests data streams from commercial and experimental hardware:

  • Hokofarm RIC (Roughage Intake Control) Troughs: Automated individual feed intake bins mounted on high-precision electronic load cells with integrated RFID cow transponder recognition. Measures start weight, end weight, duration, and eating rate per visit.
  • Trioliet Triomatic Automated Feeding Robot: Automated feeding kitchen and suspended feeding robot mixer wagon. Weighs silage, concentrates, and water during recipe loading, and logs distributed batch drops to specific barn pens and troughs.
  • SAC & Hokofarm Computerized Concentrate Stations: Automated concentrate feeding boxes dispensing up to four concentrate feeds (A, B, C, D) based on individual nutritional allowance curves.
  • GEA Automated Milking Systems: Electronic milking parlors and robotic milking units capturing individual milk yields, milking durations, quarter-level milk electrical conductivity, blood detection, and cow live body weight during milking.
  • Nedap Velos Behavior Smarttags: Accelerometer sensors (neck collars and leg pedometers) transmitting behavioral activity intervals classified into eating, ruminating, inactivity (lying), and active standing/walking.
  • C-Lock GreenFeed Emission Monitoring Units: Automated head-hood emission chambers measuring enteric methane (CH4) and carbon dioxide (CO2) flux rates during voluntary bait-fed cow visits.
  • DeLaval 3D Body Condition Score (BCS) Cameras: Overhead 3D stereoscopic imaging cameras capturing structural dorsal contours and body fat mobilization scores (scale 1.00–5.00).
  • Dairy Campus Weather Station & Airkoe Curtain Ventilation: On-site meteorological station recording ambient temperature, humidity, wind, and radiation, paired with automated barn curtain ventilation controllers.

3. Data Processing & Calculation Methodologies in SQL Tables

This section details the mathematical calculations implemented directly in the database views and stored procedures defining the 19 metadata tables.

3.1 Roughage Intake & Water Exclusion Deduction

During total mixed ration (TMR) preparation in the Trioliet Triomatic automated feeding kitchen, water is added to suppress dust and prevent ration sorting. To measure true roughage intake, the intake recorded by the Hokofarm RIC bins is adjusted by subtracting the proportion of added water logged in the mixer robot load files (view VL_Totaal_sub_ric_opn_prod):

Rwater = ∑ Mloaded − ∑ Mwater ∑ Mloaded
Iexcl_water, i = Ifresh, i × Rwater
Where Ifresh, i is the fresh roughage intake of fraction i ∈ {rv1, rv2, rv3, rv4} from Hokofarm RIC bins, Mloaded is the total ingredient batch mass loaded into the Trioliet Triomatic feeding robot, and Mwater is the mass of water added in the mixer wagon.

3.2 Laboratory Dry Matter (DM) Content & Total DMI

Weekly forage and silage samples are analyzed in the drying oven laboratory (droogstoof, VL_totaal_droogstoof) to determine dry matter percentages (function WeekGemiddelde_DS). Total daily Dry Matter Intake (DMI) across all four roughage fractions (rv1–rv4) is computed as:

DMItotal = ∑i=14 [ Iexcl_water, i × DM%, i 100 ]
Where DMItotal is the total daily dry matter intake (tot.opnDs, kg DM/day), and DM%, i is the dry matter percentage determined weekly by drying oven gravimetric analysis (VL_totaal_droogstoof via WeekGemiddelde_DS).

3.3 Concentrate Allowance vs. Consumed Intake

For SAC / Hokofarm computerized feeding boxes, programmed daily allowances (AtributedKVA–AtributedKVD) and actual consumed amounts (TotalIntakeKVA–TotalIntakeKVD) are summed across stations (view VL_Totaal_Sub_HokofarmBrok). For GEA in-parlor feeding, intake is recorded in grams (KvMlk1, KvMlk2) and converted to kg (C21-9, C21-10), while DeLaval dispensers compute:

IDelPro-13 = Qprogrammed − Qremain
Where Qprogrammed is the feed mass programmed and Qremain is the residual unconsumed feed mass remaining in the feeder (view VL_Totaal_sub_kv).

3.4 Milk Recording (MPR) AO Yield-Weighted Composite Formula

In experimental trial sampling, milk samples are collected during evening (A) and morning (O) milking sessions (AO sampling). Because milk volume and milk solids vary between morning and evening intervals, daily composite concentrations are calculated as yield-weighted averages in view VL_Dier_Datum_Crv:

C̄component = ∑j ∈ {A, O} (Cj × Yj) ∑j ∈ {A, O} Yj
Where C̄ is the 24-hour yield-weighted concentration of component C ∈ {Fat%, Protein%, Lactose%, SCC, Urea}, Cj is the concentration measured in milking session j (Evening [A] or Morning [O]), and Yj is the corresponding milking yield in kg (view VL_Dier_Datum_Crv).

Official monthly test-day records (T_Animal_MilkSamples) are stored as MlkStd (milk yield, kg), VetStd (fat %), EiwStd (protein %), LacStd (lactose %), UreStd (urea, mg/dL), CelStd (somatic cell count, 1,000 cells/mL), and KetStd (diagnostic ketosis classification code: 0 = healthy, 1 = suspect, 2 = clinical).

3.5 Nedap Velos 24-Hour Behavioral Budgets

Raw high-frequency intervals transmitted by Nedap Smarttags are categorized into behavioral states: 0 = other/active, 1 = inactive/lying, 2 = ruminating, and 3 = eating. Seconds duration are aggregated over each 24-hour calendar day in Velos_behaviour_day:

Tstate = ∑k ∈ Sstate Δtk
Where Tstate is the aggregated daily duration for behavioral state state ∈ {Eating (type 3), Ruminating (type 2), Inactive (type 1), Other active (type 0)}, and Δtk is the duration of individual observation intervals in seconds recorded by Nedap Velos Smarttags over a 24-hour cycle.

3.6 Animal Demographics & Lactation Timing

Standardized chronological and reproductive timing metrics are computed in view VL_totaal_bedrijf:

DIM = tobservation − tcalving    (Days in Milk, LacDgn)
Agedays = tobservation − tbirth    (Animal Age in Days, LftDgn)
Dateexp_calving = tinsemination + 280    (Projected Calving Date, VrwKlfDat)

3.7 Automated Data Quality & Missing Observation Matrix

View TVL_Totaal_missende_data_proef performs daily automated audit checks across all experimental cows. It flags missing milkings, missing body weights, lack of feed intake, and sensor dropouts (e.g. days where Nedap Velos logs less than 50,000 seconds of behavior).

6. Trial-Specific Data Extraction Queries

In the automated Apache Airflow pipeline (report.py), research deliverables for a specific scientific study are extracted using parameterized SQL queries. Each query filters by the unique experiment/trial code:
WHERE prfcod = '[prfcod]'

The bundled archive 04_Dairy_Campus_Calculations_and_Queries.zip contains all 30 production extraction queries (covering breeds, veterinary diagnoses, treatments, weighings, dry matter, barn climate, milking sessions, feeding visits, behavior summaries, and GreenFeed visits), along with 20 stored procedures, 190 views, and 26 user-defined functions that constitute the core calculation engine.
